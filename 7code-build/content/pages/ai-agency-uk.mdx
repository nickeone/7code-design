## BLOCK: whatWeBuild

**H2:** Four things people mean by "AI agency" — and where to go for each

Most visitors to this page want one of four things. Each has its own page with the detail, the process
and the pricing for that specific work.

- **You want manual work to stop being manual.** Intake, triage, routing, approvals, reporting,
  document handling. That is process automation with AI inside it, and it is our biggest practice.
  → [AI automation agency](/ai-automation-agency-uk)
- **You want AI features inside a product you are building or already run.** Retrieval over your own
  content, generation with guardrails, classification, search that understands intent, evals to keep
  it honest. → [AI development company](/ai-development-company-uk)
- **You want agents that take actions, not chat windows.** Systems that read a document, decide
  something, act in a real system and escalate what they should not decide alone.
  → [AI agent development](/ai-agent-development-company)
- **You want to know whether any of this is worth doing.** A senior engineering read on feasibility,
  cost, data readiness and sequencing, delivered as a document you own rather than a slide deck.
  → [AI consultancy](/ai-consultancy-uk)

And the two things underneath all four, which nobody searches for and everybody needs:

- **[System integrations](/service/system-integrations)** — the connective work that makes any of the
  above possible. APIs, middleware, data sync between tools built in different decades.
- **Production hardening** — evals, guardrails, traces, cost dashboards and rollback. This is the
  difference between a demo that impressed a board and something a business runs on.

If you are not sure which of the four you need, book the call and we will tell you. That conversation
takes twenty minutes and it is free.

## BLOCK: howWeDeliver

**H2:** How we work, and what that protects you from

- **We map before we build.** Most failed AI projects skipped this step. A use case that demos well is
  not the same as a process that is understood, and automating a broken process makes it broken faster.
- **Senior engineers only, and the same ones throughout.** The person on your first call is the person
  building it. No account manager layer, no handover to juniors once the contract is signed.
- **Model agnostic.** GPT, Claude, Gemini or an open-weight model inside your own environment. We
  choose per use case and per data-sensitivity requirement, not per vendor relationship. That also
  means we are not defending a partner tier when a cheaper model would do.
- **Evals and observability by default.** Every AI step ships with a test set, a guardrail, a trace and
  a cost dashboard. Non-deterministic software without measurement is not production software.
- **You own everything.** Code, prompts, eval suites, infrastructure config and documentation, running
  in your cloud account. Nothing here requires us to keep existing.
- **Your data stays in the EU.** We are an EU company delivering from Romania. Data residency, GDPR
  posture and a DPA are settled before the first line of code, not after a security questionnaire
  arrives.

## BLOCK: pointOfView

**H2:** Our point of view

"AI agency" is a label the market has not agreed on, and the ambiguity is doing real damage to buyers.
A firm that runs your paid social and a firm that ships a retrieval pipeline into production are not
the same kind of business. They need different skills, different contracts and different people in the
room.

Our view is that AI work is software engineering. The model is one component in a system that also
needs data plumbing, error handling, permissions, monitoring, rollback and a cost ceiling. Teams that
treat the model as the whole product ship impressive demos and then spend a year discovering the other
eighty percent.

We would rather be described narrowly and accurately than broadly and vaguely. So: we build AI
software. We advise on it when advice is what is needed, and we say no when the honest answer is that
a spreadsheet and one fixed integration would solve the problem for a tenth of the cost.

**Who we are not for:** if you are looking for AI-assisted marketing, content generation at volume, or
campaign automation, we are the wrong firm and we will say so on the first call rather than stretching
to fit. We build software. Good marketing agencies exist and we are not one.

## BLOCK: faq

**H2:** Questions we get asked before we're hired

**1. What does working with an AI agency actually cost?** `objection: price`
It depends on scope, but these are the bands we work in. A process audit and opportunity map, the
[Operational Quick Scan](/packages/operational-quick-scan), is typically **€4,500–€6,000** over two to
three weeks. A deeper readiness and sequencing piece, the
[AI-Ready Ops Blueprint](/packages/ai-ready-ops-blueprint), usually sits between **€9,000 and €14,000**
over four to six weeks. Building one workflow end to end, an
[Automation Pilot](/packages/automation-pilot), typically runs **€15,000–€25,000** over six to ten
weeks. A full AI product build, the [Production MVP](/mvp-development-agency-uk), is usually
**€40,000–€90,000** over three to five months. What moves the number is the number of systems involved
and how clean your data is. We break it down per workflow during the scan so you can drop anything
that does not pay for itself. More detail in
[what AI automation costs](/hub/what-ai-automation-costs-uk).

**2. Are you a marketing agency that does AI?** `objection: other`
No. We are a software engineering firm. We do not run campaigns, buy media, write ad copy or manage
your content calendar. What we build is software with AI inside it: automated workflows, agents,
retrieval systems, AI features inside products. If your need is marketing-side, we will tell you on
the first call instead of taking the project.

**3. Do you build it yourselves, or subcontract?** `objection: seniority`
We build it. The engineers are employed by us, in Cluj-Napoca, and the person you meet on the first
call is on the build. There is no delivery partner behind the curtain and no account manager layer
between you and the people writing the code. You can meet the engineer before you commit, and we would
rather you did.

**4. Which of your pages should I actually be on?** `objection: other`
If the problem is manual operational work, go to the
[AI automation agency](/ai-automation-agency-uk) page. If you are adding AI to a product,
[AI development company](/ai-development-company-uk). If you want systems that take actions rather
than answer questions, [AI agent development](/ai-agent-development-company). If you need a decision before
a build, [AI consultancy](/ai-consultancy-uk). If none of those quite fit, book the call and describe
the problem in plain terms; routing you correctly takes about twenty minutes.

**5. How long until we see something working?** `objection: timeline`
The scan produces a written deliverable in two to three weeks. A first automated workflow is usually
live six to ten weeks from kickoff. A full product build runs three to five months. We deliberately
pick an early win your team can watch working, rather than disappearing for two quarters and returning
with a platform. See [how we work](/process) for the sequence.

**6. We tried an AI tool last year and it did not work. Why would this be different?** `objection: failed-before`
You are not unusual, and the cause is usually the same. Someone picked a use case because it demoed
well, not because the process behind it was understood. It worked on clean sample data and fell over
on the real thing. We map the process first and test against your actual data before you commit to a
build. If it will not work, you find that out in week two for the price of a scan.

**7. Will we be locked into you, or into one AI vendor?** `objection: lock-in`
Neither. You own the code, the prompts, the eval suites and the infrastructure configuration, and it
runs in your cloud account. Everything is documented so another team can pick it up. We are model
agnostic, so swapping providers is a configuration change rather than a rebuild. Some clients keep us
on for the next workflow; plenty take it in house after the first project, which is a perfectly good
outcome.

**8. Who owns the IP?** `objection: ip-ownership`
You do, transferring on full payment. That includes anything we build on top of our own internal
tooling, and it covers code, prompts, evals and documentation. It is in our standard contract, not a
paid upgrade, and we will sign your NDA and MSA if you have your own.

**9. Our data is sensitive. Where does it go?** `objection: data-residency`
We are an EU company delivering from Romania, so data stays inside the EEA by default and a DPA is part
of standard paperwork. Where data cannot leave your environment at all, we run open-weight models
inside your own infrastructure rather than calling a hosted API. Engineers normally work in your cloud
accounts under your access controls. We settle all of this during the scan, before any build starts.

**10. What happens after launch?** `objection: after-launch`
Everything ships with a test set, guardrails, traces and a cost dashboard, so your team can see it
working and see it drift. We offer a support retainer, but it is optional and it is not how we make a
project viable. If you want to run it yourself from day one, the handover documentation is written on
that assumption.

**11. What if we need engineers rather than a project?** `objection: sideline-our-team`
Then we do that too. Where a team already has direction and is simply short of senior capacity, our
[staff augmentation](/staff-augmentation-company-uk) model puts engineers into your repo and your
standups on a monthly rolling basis. It is a different commercial shape from a fixed-scope build, and
sometimes it is the honest answer. We will tell you which one fits on the first call.

**12. Do you work with UK companies, and where are you based?** `objection: other`
Yes. We work with companies across the UK, Ireland, the Nordics, Benelux and DACH, and we deliver from
Cluj-Napoca in Romania. That is two hours ahead of London, so the working day overlaps almost
entirely and projects run in English. Most engagements start and continue over video, and we travel to
the UK for kickoffs and planning where it helps.
